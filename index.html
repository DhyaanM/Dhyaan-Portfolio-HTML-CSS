Mahatma Gandhi

The man who led India to it's freedom

Here's a time-line of Mahatma Gandhi's life:

  • October 2, 1869: Birth of Mahatma Gandhi in Porbandar, India 🎂

  • 1883: Gandhi and Kasturbai are married 💖

  • 1885: Death of Gandhi's father 🕊️

  • 1888: Gandhi goes to England to study law 📖

  • 1891: Gandhi passes the bar exam in England 🎓

  • 1893: Gandhi accepts commission to spend a year in South Africa advising on a lawsuit ✈️

  • 1896: Gandhi returns to India to collect his wife and children ✈️

  • 1896: Gandhi goes back to South Africa with his family ✈️

  • 1899: Outbreak of Boer War (1899-1901) in South Africa 🗡️

  • 1914: World War I (1914-1918) 🗺️

  • 1947: India's and Pakistan's independence 😊

  • January 30, 1948: Gandhi was shot by Nathuram Godse while on his way to a prayer gathering in New Delhi 🕊️
Fun Facts About Gandhi
  • He was married at the young age of 13!
  • His real name is Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi
  • He suffered from stage fright
  • He never actually got a Nobel Peace Prize
  • His birthday (October 2nd) is also known as International Non-Violence Day
